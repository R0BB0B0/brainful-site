How I can help

Bring me the challenge. I'll shape the work to fit.

Expert facilitation

Leadership offsites, branch planning days, community consultation and co-design workshops. Days that build trust early and carry the group through to concrete outputs, with capture and reporting afterwards.

Learning and program design

Experiential programs people learn by doing, designed from the theory of change through to delivery and evaluation. Built for professional cohorts who have sat through plenty of generic training.

AI literacy for leaders and teams

A clear, accurate picture of what AI changes about how people work. The missing capability is knowing when to trust the output and when to question it. Tool tips come second.

Community and stakeholder engagement

Consultation designed around what each group needs to say and be heard on, including First Nations and cross-jurisdictional engagement.

How I work

Practice led, evidence based

Start with the real problem

Organisations are often stuck somewhere other than where they think. I'd rather hear the actual challenge than sell you a package.

Learn by doing

Hands-on, gameful activities followed by honest reflection. Information on its own rarely changes behaviour. Culture and norms do.

Design for the return to work

A great day matters little if the workplace pulls people back to the old way. The ideas and practices have to travel home with them.

Stay small, prove efficacy, scale in context

As an action researcher I test, measure and adjust while the work runs, then grow what holds up.

Who I work with

Government at every level, Defence, and the organisations that work alongside them. Over the years that has included ACT Parks, the Department of Veterans' Affairs, Comcare, Defence Cyber Forces, the Royal Australian Air Force and Questacon.
