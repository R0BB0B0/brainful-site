Hi, I'm Rob.

Experience designer. Facilitator. Speed demon. I'm the bridge between the expertise in the ivory tower and the real world.

The long way round

From the clinic to cybernetics

A healthy relationship with risk and an injury-laden youth led me to medical science. I was a country kid from Tumut, raised by a science teacher and an outdoor educator. Curiosity, meaningful connection and the outdoors ran in the family.

I started as a respiratory scientist at Canberra Hospital, working with people on some of their worst days, including children with cystic fibrosis. It taught me empathy. It also taught me to translate complex ideas into plain language that suits the context and the people in front of me.

Someone noticed how I explained things and pointed me towards science communication. My Masters research showed that when data touches who people are, their ability to read it drops. So I design around what people care about as well as what they need to know.

I spent over a decade at Questacon. I coordinated national touring programs, developed exhibition concepts and managed programs in the Centre. I also coordinated Inspiring Australia's national network, which runs science engagement year round across the country and National Science Week each August. Questacon taught me to design for how people think, feel and act. The space, the artefacts and the way something is presented all shape the experience, so every choice needs intention.

Leading EDGY, the Air Force's bottom-up innovation program, taught me how culture shifts in a complex organisation. One-off training fades when people return to a job where nothing has changed. Change holds when peers, leaders and the system around people support it. In complexity there is no straight line, so you probe, learn and adapt as you go.

Co-founding Veracity Innovation taught me to shape the work to what a client really needs, and to stay steady in uncertainty.

Today I'm the Learning Experiences Convenor at the ANU School of Cybernetics. Cybernetics gives me a disciplinary foundation for working with complexity. How a system is organised, what it rewards and how feedback moves through it all shape what people do. You steer by watching what happens and correcting course.

I bring behaviour change, human-centred design, systems thinking and how people learn together as one lens. When a job needs it, I bring in trusted specialists.

I founded Brainful to bring that whole journey to people who are trying to solve real problems. We only get one life. I choose meaning.
